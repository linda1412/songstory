Electricity


Electricity [Note:Electricity is a form of energy that produces heat,light,and power.] is produced in power plants and carried to your home via the electric power grid.

Nonrenewable Energy Resources


Nonrenewable energy sources are those that exist in a limited supply and cannot be replaced or replenished as quickly as they are used.There are two main types of nonrenewable energy sources:
  • 1. Fossil Fuels: Fossil fuels (coal,petroleum/crude oil,and natural gas) formed from the buried remains of plants and animals that lived millions of years ago.

  • 2. Nuclear Energy: Nuclear power is energy in the nucleus (core) of an atom. Splitting atoms produces heat, which makes electricity, in a nuclear power plant.

Renewable Energy Resources


The most common renewable energy sources are
  1. Hydroelectric power, or hydropower,uses flowing water to turn turbines to produce electricity.
  2. Solar energy uses a varity of technologies to convert sunlight to usable energy. Energy from the sun can be used to heart water,heat or cool air, and generate electricity for individual homes or bulidings. On a large scale, it can be used to produce electricity to power cities and small towns.
  3. Wind energy uses air blowing across propeller blades causing them to spin to produce electricity.

电


电 【注：电是一种产生热、光和动力的能量形式。】 是在发电厂生产的，通过电网输送到家。

不可再生能源


不可再生能源是指那些供应有限且无法在使用时快速更换或补充的能源。不可再生能源主要有两种：
  • 1. 化石燃料：化石燃料是 （煤炭、石油/原油和天然气）由数百万年前埋藏的动植物遗骸形成的。

  • 2.核能：核能是原子核（核心）中的能量。在核电站中，分裂原子产生热量，然后发电。

可再生能源


最常见的可再生能源包括：
  1. 水力发电, 或 称水电,利用流动的水转动涡轮机来发电。
  2. 太阳能 利用多种技术将阳光转化为可利用的能源。 太阳能可用于加热水、加热或冷却空气，并为家庭或建筑物发电。 在更大规模上，它可用于发电，为城市和小镇提供电力。
  3. 风能 利用空气吹过螺旋桨叶片 使其旋转来发电。
